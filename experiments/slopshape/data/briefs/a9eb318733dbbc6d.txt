Write a blog post for the publisher, a provider of companion care services offered to health plans as a supplemental member benefit, arguing that health care navigation support is the key to closing preventive care gaps among older Medicare Advantage members. Aim it at plan administrators puzzled by low annual wellness visit rates. Explain the real barriers: underestimating prevention, physical and cognitive limitations, confusion over benefits, and weak digital literacy. Connect these gaps to avoidable chronic disease costs and hospital use,. Then show how companion care professionals help members understand coverage, schedule appointments, get transportation, access telehealth, and act as the plan's eyes and ears. Keep the tone businesslike yet humane, closing on the dual value to plan and member.
