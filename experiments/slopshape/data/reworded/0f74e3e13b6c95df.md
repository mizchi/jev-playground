## Standout Features of the OEC 9900 Elite

GE built the 9900 Elite to produce high-quality images while giving clinicians flexibility and control. Its most valuable features include:

**Motion-tolerant subtraction imaging.** In vascular work, a major strength of the 9900 Elite is that it produces clear subtraction images even when the patient moves somewhat. Motion artifacts can degrade traditional digital subtraction angiography, which leads to repeat runs and extra contrast. Motion-tolerant subtraction cuts down on those artifacts, which supports cleaner views of vessels and may reduce contrast and dose.

**High-resolution imaging chain.** A 1K x 1K imaging chain paired with a high-performance CCD camera gives sharp, detailed images of fine anatomical structures and devices.

**Rotating anode X-ray tube.** The rotating anode tube handles longer procedures and higher-power imaging, and its dual focal spots add flexibility for different clinical applications.

**Advanced image processing.** Real-time noise reduction, edge enhancement, roadmapping, and digital pulsed fluoroscopy help clinicians weigh image quality against radiation dose.

**Intuitive workstation.** With dual high-resolution monitors, an easy-to-use control panel, and plenty of image storage, clinicians can review, compare, and archive images during and after procedures.

**Maneuverability.** Because the C-arm is compact, it positions easily around the patient and table, and it offers a wide range of orbital and lateral rotation.

## Available Configurations

The 9900 Elite platform comes in several configurations, each suited to particular clinical needs.

### Vascular (Endovascular) Configuration

This configuration is intended for interventional and endovascular procedures. It adds enhanced subtraction capabilities, roadmapping, and vascular-specific software packages for procedures like angioplasty, stenting, and peripheral interventions. Here, motion-tolerant subtraction is particularly useful.

### Cardiac Configuration

The cardiac configuration serves procedures that need high frame rates and exceptional temporal resolution. Its cardiac-specific imaging modes and cine capabilities support pacemaker implantation, electrophysiology procedures, and other cardiac interventions that depend on capturing motion clearly.

### Motorized Configuration

On the motorized 9900 Elite, key axes of the C-arm move under motor control, so clinicians can position the system precisely from the control panel or tableside. Staff reposition the unit by hand less often, which helps preserve sterility and smooths workflow in complex cases.

### Navigation Configuration

The navigation configuration integrates with surgical navigation systems, especially for orthopedic and spine surgery. It supports image-guided workflows so surgeons can plan and carry out procedures more accurately.

- A refreshed workstation and user interface
- More image storage and improved connectivity
- A wider range of specialized configurations

Facilities that perform vascular, cardiac, or other image-intensive procedures often find the 9900 Elite well worth the investment.
