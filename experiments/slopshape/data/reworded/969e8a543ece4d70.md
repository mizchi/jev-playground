# 3 Recruitment Trends Shaping 2023 and How an ATS Turns Them Into Advantages

Recruitment has changed more over the past few years than it did in the ten years before. Candidates ask more of employers, teams work across cities and time zones, and screening résumés by degree and job title no longer finds the right people.

HR professionals and recruitment agencies that still depend on spreadsheets, email threads, and outdated software are struggling to keep pace. These shifts are more than problems, though. With the right tools, they let you hire faster and make better hires.

This article covers three of the biggest recruitment trends of 2023, the challenge each creates, and how an applicant tracking system (ATS) such as Manatal can help your team turn them into competitive advantages.

## Trend 1: Rising Expectations Around Employee Well-Being

Employee well-being is no longer optional; it's now a top priority. Following years of disruption, burnout, and uncertainty, workers put much more weight on mental health, work-life balance, and support from their employers.

Salary and title are no longer the only questions candidates ask. They want to know how a company treats its people: whether it provides mental health resources, whether workloads are reasonable, whether there's room for personal growth, and whether the culture is supportive or toxic.

Employers with convincing answers have a clear advantage. Employers without them are finding top talent harder to attract and even harder to retain.

## Trend 2: Growing Demand for Hybrid and Remote Work

Remote work isn't going away. For many professionals, an arrangement that started as an emergency measure is now a permanent expectation. Surveys consistently find that a large share of workers prefer hybrid or fully remote setups, and many would think about quitting a job that demanded a full-time return to the office.

This gives employers new options. Hiring is no longer confined to people within commuting distance; you can recruit from talent pools in other regions and even other countries.

Remote and hybrid work also change the mechanics of recruitment. Hiring managers, recruiters, and interviewers may never share a room, so scheduling, feedback, and decisions all have to work smoothly at a distance.

## Trend 3: The Shift Toward Skills-Based Hiring

For a long time, hiring has leaned on proxies such as degrees, previous employers, and years of experience. A growing number of organizations now recognize that these signals don't reliably predict how someone will perform.

Skills-based hiring looks at what candidates can actually do: write clean code, manage a complex project, or communicate clearly with clients. It enlarges the talent pool, opens doors for people from non-traditional backgrounds, and often produces better hires.

Major employers have already dropped degree requirements for many roles, and others are following. For recruiters, though, this calls for more nuanced evaluation, which is hard when there are hundreds of applications to review.
