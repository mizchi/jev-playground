# Why We Love January (Yes, Really!)

> "In the depth of winter, I finally learned that within me there lay an invincible summer."
> — Albert Camus

People don't think much of January. The holiday lights get packed away, the days are short, the wind stings, and December's credit card statement lands about as gently as a snowplow. No surprise, then, that plenty of people just try to get through the month.

We'd like to defend it. Beneath the gray skies and frosted windshields, January has more charm than it gets credit for. Pour yourself something warm, put on your softest silk pajamas, and let us walk you through what we love about it.

## A Fresh Start (and a Clean Slate)

A brand-new calendar always feels hopeful. January takes its name from Janus, the Roman god of beginnings and transitions, who was shown with two faces, one looking back at the past and one looking toward the future. It's the natural month to stop, think about the year you've just had, and do a little dreaming about the one ahead.

It's also resolution season. Perhaps you've promised yourself you'll drink more water, read more books, or finally sort out that closet. Good for you! And if your resolutions have quietly disappeared by the third week of January, you're far from alone. The second Friday in January is sometimes jokingly called "Quitter's Day," because so many people abandon their goals around then. Our suggestion: make your resolution to go easy on yourself. That's a much easier one to keep!

## Winter Babies, Unite!

Born in January? Congratulations, you're in interesting company. Some studies have suggested a link between the season of your birth and certain personality traits and temperaments. Winter babies have been described as less prone to extreme mood swings than people born in other seasons, and January's Capricorns and Aquarians have a reputation for being determined, independent, and endlessly original.

Whether you put your faith in science, the stars, or neither, we say January birthdays earn an extra slice of cake. Throwing a party in the dead of winter takes real commitment!

## The Joy of Being a Homebody

Between us, January is the month when staying in is allowed. The holiday parties are over, the social calendar is empty, and nobody expects you anywhere. It's just you, a cozy blanket, and everything that makes hunkering down so pleasant.

A few ideas:

- Curling up with the novel you've meant to read for ages
- Binge-watching the series everyone keeps recommending
- Lighting candles and soaking in a long, luxurious bath
- Sleeping late on a snowy Saturday morning
- Spending the whole day in your favorite silk robe (why not?)

Bears aren't the only ones allowed to hibernate, darling. Call it self-care!

## Comfort Food Season Is Here

January beats every other month at comfort food. It's the season for bubbling casseroles, stews that simmer for hours, crusty bread straight out of the oven, and big mugs of hot cocoa piled high with whipped cream.

Our favorite fun fact: January is National Soup Month! Chicken noodle traditionalist, creamy tomato devotee, or spicy tortilla soup adventurer, you now have the perfect excuse to make your kitchen smell of something warm and nourishing. Make a big pot, invite a few friends, and throw a soup night. You won't host a cozier party.

## Meanwhile, Down Under...

Missing the sun? Here's something to warm you up: in the southern hemisphere, January is summer! While we scrape ice off our cars, people in Australia, New Zealand, South Africa, Argentina, and Brazil are enjoying the sunshine, going to the beach, and lighting the barbecue.

If the winter blues are wearing you down, this could be the moment to book that tropical getaway. Or at least daydream about one over a bowl of soup!

## Warm Feet, Happy Heart

We have one more January secret we can't keep to ourselves. Those fuzzy socks you've worn all month may be doing more for you than you realize!

According to a study from the Netherlands, couples who wore socks to bed were more likely to reach, let's say, a satisfying finish in the bedroom. Warm feet apparently help the body relax, and a relaxed body is a very happy one. Who knew?

So pair those cozy socks with your silkiest nightie, dim the lights, and let January do the rest. Your secret is safe with us. Wink!

## Tell Us: What Do You Love About January?

January is cold and dark, yes, but it also brings fresh starts, cozy nights, hearty meals, and a few pleasant surprises. We think it's earned a bit more affection!

Now it's your turn. What do you love most about January: a special tradition, a favorite soup recipe, a winter birthday, or just the pleasure of staying in? Tell us in the comments below. We're looking forward to reading them!
