# 3 Recruitment Trends Shaping 2023 and How an ATS Turns Them Into Advantages

The world of recruitment has changed more in the past few years than in the decade before. Candidates expect more from employers, teams are spread across cities and time zones, and the old way of filtering résumés by degree and job title is no longer enough to find the right people.

For HR professionals and recruitment agencies still relying on spreadsheets, email threads, and outdated software, keeping up can feel like an uphill battle. The good news? These shifts aren't just challenges. With the right tools, they're opportunities to hire faster, smarter, and better than ever.

In this article, we'll explore three of the biggest recruitment trends shaping 2023, the challenges each one brings, and how an applicant tracking system (ATS) like Manatal can help your team turn them into real competitive advantages.

## Trend 1: Rising Expectations Around Employee Well-Being

Employee well-being has moved from a nice-to-have to a top priority. After years of disruption, burnout, and uncertainty, workers are placing far greater value on mental health, work-life balance, and feeling supported by their employers.

Candidates today aren't just asking about salary and title. They want to know how a company treats its people. Does it offer mental health resources? Are workloads reasonable? Is there room for personal growth? Is the culture supportive or toxic?

Employers who can answer those questions convincingly have a clear edge. Those who can't are finding it harder to attract top talent, and harder still to keep it.

## Trend 2: Growing Demand for Hybrid and Remote Work

Remote work is here to stay. What began as an emergency response has become a lasting expectation for many professionals. Surveys consistently show that a large share of workers prefer hybrid or fully remote arrangements, and many would consider leaving a job that required them to return to the office full-time.

For employers, this shift opens up exciting possibilities. You're no longer limited to candidates within commuting distance. You can tap into talent pools across regions and even countries.

But remote and hybrid work also change how recruitment happens. Hiring managers, recruiters, and interviewers may never be in the same room. Coordinating schedules, sharing feedback, and making decisions all need to happen smoothly across distance.

## Trend 3: The Shift Toward Skills-Based Hiring

Traditional hiring has long relied on proxies: degrees, previous employers, and years of experience. But more and more organizations are realizing that these signals don't always predict success on the job.

Skills-based hiring focuses on what candidates can actually do. Can they write clean code? Manage a complex project? Communicate clearly with clients? This approach widens the talent pool, gives opportunities to people from non-traditional backgrounds, and often leads to better hires.

Major employers have already removed degree requirements from many roles, and the trend is spreading. For recruiters, however, it means evaluating candidates in a more nuanced way, and that can be difficult when you're reviewing hundreds of applications.
