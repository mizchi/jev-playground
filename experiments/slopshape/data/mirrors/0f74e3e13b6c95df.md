## Standout Features of the OEC 9900 Elite

The 9900 Elite was designed to deliver high-quality images while giving clinicians flexibility and control. Some of its most valuable features include:

**Motion-tolerant subtraction imaging.** One of the 9900 Elite's key advantages in vascular work is its ability to produce clear subtraction images even when there is some patient movement. Traditional digital subtraction angiography can be degraded by motion artifacts, forcing repeat runs and additional contrast. Motion-tolerant subtraction helps reduce those artifacts, supporting cleaner vessel visualization and potentially lowering contrast and dose.

**High-resolution imaging chain.** The system uses a 1K x 1K imaging chain with a high-performance CCD camera to deliver sharp, detailed images for fine anatomical structures and devices.

**Rotating anode X-ray tube.** A rotating anode tube supports longer procedures and higher-power imaging, with dual focal spots for flexibility across different clinical applications.

**Advanced image processing.** Features such as real-time noise reduction, edge enhancement, roadmapping, and digital pulsed fluoroscopy help clinicians balance image quality and radiation dose.

**Intuitive workstation.** Dual high-resolution monitors, a user-friendly control panel, and ample image storage make it easy to review, compare, and archive images during and after procedures.

**Maneuverability.** The compact C-arm design allows easy positioning around the patient and table, with a wide range of orbital and lateral rotation.

## Available Configurations

The 9900 Elite platform is available in several configurations tailored to specific clinical needs.

### Vascular (Endovascular) Configuration

The vascular configuration is built for interventional and endovascular procedures. It includes enhanced subtraction capabilities, roadmapping, and vascular-specific software packages that support procedures such as angioplasty, stenting, and peripheral interventions. Motion-tolerant subtraction is especially valuable in this setting.

### Cardiac Configuration

The cardiac configuration is designed for procedures requiring high frame rates and exceptional temporal resolution. With cardiac-specific imaging modes and cine capabilities, it supports pacemaker implantation, electrophysiology procedures, and other cardiac interventions where capturing motion clearly is essential.

### Motorized Configuration

The motorized 9900 Elite adds motorized movement to key axes of the C-arm, allowing clinicians to position the system with precision from the control panel or tableside. This reduces the need for manual repositioning, helps maintain sterility, and improves workflow during complex cases.

### Navigation Configuration

The navigation configuration is designed to integrate with surgical navigation systems, particularly in orthopedic and spine surgery. It supports image-guided workflows that help surgeons plan and execute procedures with greater accuracy.

## OEC 9900 Elite vs. OEC 9800

Many facilities considering the 9900 Elite are also looking at GE's older OEC 9800. The 9800 is a proven system, but the 9900 Elite offers meaningful upgrades, including:

- Improved image quality through a more advanced imaging chain and processing
- Enhanced subtraction imaging with better motion tolerance
- Updated workstation and user interface
- Greater image storage and improved connectivity
- A broader range of specialized configurations

For facilities performing vascular, cardiac, or other image-intensive procedures, the 9900 Elite is often well worth the investment.
